# Business Intelligence (BI)

This repository is dedicated to learning and exploring **Business Intelligence (BI)**, including its concepts, tools, techniques, and practical applications.

It will contain notes, examples, code, projects, and resources covering different areas of Business Intelligence, such as:

- **Data Analysis:** Collecting, exploring, and analysing data to identify meaningful insights.
- **Data Visualisation:** Presenting data through charts, graphs, and dashboards.
- **Data Warehousing:** Understanding how data is stored, organised, and managed.
- **ETL (Extract, Transform, Load):** Learning how data is extracted, transformed, and loaded for analysis.
- **Reporting:** Turning data into meaningful reports that support business decisions.
- **BI Tools:** Exploring tools such as Power BI, Tableau, and SQL.

The goal of this repository is to build a strong understanding of Business Intelligence, from fundamental concepts to practical, real-world applications, and continuously document the learning journey.
